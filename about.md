## What this site covers

The main thread of this site is the **NPAt format** — an alternative numerical
representation to IEEE 754, developed and documented across several open
repositories (see the [home page]({{ "/" | relative_url }}) for the full
list).

Alongside NPAt, I also publish shorter notes and reflections on numerical
computing more broadly: things that come up in research, in correspondence,
or in discussions elsewhere (including notes originally written for Quora)
that don't necessarily tie back to NPAt directly, but are part of the same
general interest in how numbers are represented and computed.
